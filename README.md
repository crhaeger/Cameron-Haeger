I think having both school and management experience has helped me better understand how some of the concepts I learn in class can apply to an actual business.
As I continue my finance degree, I want to learn more about investing, trading, financial analysis, and the different career opportunities available within finance. After graduating, I hope to find a career where I can use my interest in finance and analytics while continuing to develop my skills and gain experience. Through this portfolio, I hope to show my growth as a finance student, the projects I have completed, and the experience I have gained from balancing school with a management position.
